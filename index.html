What a token must pass

  • Liquidity of at least $25,000 (money sitting in the trading pool)
  • At least $10,000 traded in the last 24 hours
  • Trading pair at least 24 hours old
  • At least 100 trades in the last 24 hours
  • Not many buys with almost no sells (a honeypot sign)
  • Liquidity at least 2% of market cap (harder to drain)

Scans run about every 15 minutes. These rules are in rules.py, and a token that passes them can still lose money or be a rug.
